Safari installation

Add Grensen 18 to your iPhone or iPad

Keep this guide open and complete the steps using Safari's controls.

  1. Tap the three-dot More button at the bottom-right in Safari beside the address field, or tap the Share button below. If you use Share, continue at step 3.
  2. Tap Share.
  3. Tap Show More at the bottom-right.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen.
  5. Tap Add at the top-right.

The Grensen 18 icon appearing on your Home Screen confirms that installation succeeded.
